Track Market Average Price Movement

Track Price Movement with easily accessible market average price trends for thousands of products and services and analyze percentage changes in price for desired time ranges.

Compare Against My Prices

Leverage ANKLESARIA’s groundbreaking Competitive Advantage Measurement System (CAMS™) methodology to

  • Compare trends of historical and current prices paid to suppliers against market average price trends.
  • Assess competitiveness of prices paid to suppliers and quantify the advantage or disadvantage of the prices v/s the market.
  • Evaluate competitiveness of price proposals submitted by suppliers.

Cost Intelligence

Understand cost to effectively Manage cost.

Industry Cost Profiles (ICP)

Generate Industry Cost Profiles (ICP) for Manufacturing and Service Industries to understand the average percentage breakdown of price into its subsequent cost elements. Estimate ICPs for large enterprises as well as small and medium enterprises.

Estimate critical cost elements and their cost drivers

  • Generate comprehensive Bills of Materials (BOM) for products and Statements of Work (SOW) for services.
  • Estimate cost drivers like unit prices, waste factors and required quantities to quantify direct material costs.
  • Estimate cost drivers like wage rates, utilization factors and required hours to quantify direct labor costs.

Should Cost Analysis.

Generate automated Should Cost analysis using ANKLESARIA’s tried and tested should cost modelling process for thousands of products and services.
